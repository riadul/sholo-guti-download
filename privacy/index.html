Sholo Guti: privacy policy

Published by Abacus IT (abacusitbd.com, info@abacusitbd.com). Last updated 18 November 2026.

  • The game collects no personal data. There are no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your player names, statistics, match history, settings and saved games stay on your own device only. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • Online play (with a friend by room code) sends only what the game needs to your friend through our small meeting point: the room code, the name and picture you chose in the game, and your moves. Nothing is stored; a room is gone when the game ends.
  • The game asks for no permissions apart from internet access (for online play) and, on phones, vibration.
  • The game is suitable for everyone, including children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Questions: info@abacusitbd.com
